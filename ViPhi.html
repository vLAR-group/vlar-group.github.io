ViPhi: Workshop and Challenge on Visual Physics Learning and Reasoning

Advancing Physical World Understanding, Generation, Simulation, and Embodied AI

🤗 PhysInOne Dataset 🏆 PhysInOne Challenge

Seattle, USA · June 20 or 21, 2027 (Half Day, Tentative)

Abstract

At its core, visual scenes are more than appearance or geometry. They are active reflections of the physical nature of our world. Unlocking this physical essence within visual content represents a crucial milestone for artificial intelligence, driving the transition from passive observation to actionable, physically grounded reasoning.

The ViPhi workshop unites researchers across computer vision, machine learning, and robotics to anchor physics learning from visual inputs. Our scope spans physics-grounded 3D and 4D modelling, physics-aware video generation, future frame prediction, physical property estimation, motion transfer, physical fidelity benchmarking, and physics-aware robotic manipulation, paving the way for physically grounded, actionable AI.

Workshop Topics

Physics-Grounded 3D/4D World Models

Integrating physical dynamics into neural or explicit spatial representations (e.g., dynamic Gaussian Splatting) to capture physical response over time.

Physics-Aware Video Generation

Injecting explicit physical principles into generative video models to eradicate unphysical artifacts like ungrounded acceleration and object penetration.

Future Frame Prediction

Extrapolating short-term contact dynamics and long-term scene trajectories in 3D/4D spaces adhering to physical conservation laws.

Physical Properties Estimation

Recovering unobservable intrinsic properties (mass, friction, elasticity, stiffness, density) straight from visual cues for advanced physical reasoning.

Motion Transfer

Transferring complex motion patterns across heterogeneous shapes respecting realistic kinematics, force propagation, and contact boundaries.

Metrics & Physical Fidelity Benchmarking

Establishing rigorous quantitative evaluation protocols and datasets dedicated to measuring physical plausibility beyond standard PSNR/FVD.

Official Competition Evaluation Server Ready

PhysInOne Challenge

We host the PhysInOne Challenge utilizing the large-scale multi-physics dataset collected and annotated by our group. All training data is publicly available on Hugging Face, evaluated via a dedicated private test split.

Track 1

Physics-Aware Video Generation

Evaluating temporal smoothness and real-world mechanics compliance.

Track 2

Future Frame Prediction

Benchmarking predictive capabilities across short-term and long-term horizons.

Track 3

Physical Properties Estimation

Testing inverse physics performance to recover stiffness, viscosity, and friction.

Track 4

Motion Transfer

Assessing fidelity of transferring complex multi-physics motions to novel targets.
