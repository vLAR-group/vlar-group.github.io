A stone pedestal holds a pool of liquid, its surface calm and reflective. Resting atop the liquid is a small comb, its teeth gently touching the fluid’s edge. Suddenly, the comb slips, falling into the liquid with a soft splash. Gravity pulls it downward, displacing the fluid and sending ripples outward in concentric waves. The liquid swirls momentarily around the submerged comb before settling back, its surface slowly regaining stillness as gravity restores equilibrium.
