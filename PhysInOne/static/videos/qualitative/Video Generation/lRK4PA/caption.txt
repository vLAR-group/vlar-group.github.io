Three yellow rubber ducks float in a bathtub, each oriented differently, bobbing gently as water flows downward. The current exerts dynamic forces on their surfaces, causing them to sway and rotate in response to fluid motion. Buoyancy counteracts gravity, keeping them afloat while subtle shifts in water pressure generate continuous, rhythmic movements. Their positions change slightly over time, influenced by the interplay of gravitational pull and the stream’s momentum, creating a quiet, fluid dance within the tub.
